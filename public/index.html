How to play

  1. HOLD (touch, mouse or SPACE) to push your orb away from the black hole. RELEASE and gravity pulls you in.
  2. Move closer = faster. Move out = slower. Use it to catch the glowing shards ◆.
  3. Touching the black hole or the red edge knocks you out for 2s. The edge shrinks in the last 30 seconds!
  4. Bump your rival — if they fall into the red soon after, you get a KO +2.
  5. Most points after 90 seconds wins. Tied? Sudden death.
